Interpretation:
The results suggest that hyperparameter performance is strongly context-dependent.
Each context achieves its best score with its own native configuration,
and no single configuration generalizes best across all contexts.
This is especially visible for the high_gravity setting:
the configuration tuned for high_gravity performs very well in its own context, 
but transfers poorly to low_gravity and normal. 
In contrast, the low_gravity and normal configurations 
transfer somewhat better to each other, 
which indicates that these two contexts are more similar from the optimizer’s perspective.

Overall, the experiment reproduces the main observation of the paper:
hyperparameters in contextual RL are highly situational. 
A configuration that is optimal in one context may become clearly suboptimal in another,
so tuning should ideally be performed with the target context in mind rather than assuming 
that one globally good configuration exists.
