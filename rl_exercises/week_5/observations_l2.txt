### Observations 1:
Short trajectories (50 steps) produced low and noisy eval rewards around 35–50. The 100-step runs were the most stable and reached 100 reward consistently by 100 episodes. Longer trajectories (200 and 500) still learn, but training became less predictable and more seed-dependent.
